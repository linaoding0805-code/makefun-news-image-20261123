One image that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026, built
for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
[news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/competitions/news-image-20261123).

The front page (`entry.png`, 1024 x 1024) leads with the Russian drone strike
that flattened a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv — the
toll rose to 11 on Monday as the search ended, with a boy of seven and a woman
of 79 pulled alive from the rubble. It also carries Ukraine's claim that seven
sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, with
satellite images showing the hull smoldering and partly submerged at the pier; Indonesia's
confirmation of 24 dead and about 30 missing after a blaze tore through a
confectionery factory in Cikande, Banten; and Wall Street's sharp rebound, led
by Nvidia's surge on Jensen Huang's return as chief executive, with the day's
other stories below the fold.

## How it is made

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` and verifies the copy byte for byte. The page itself was
composed in the build session from four AI illustrations drawn for the day's
reported events; the pictures are labelled as illustrations on the page.
