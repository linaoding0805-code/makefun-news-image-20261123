## Claim 2 — Merkury / Angara — PARTIAL

Page text: "Zelenskyy says seven sea drones sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi and hit the cargo ship Angara; satellite images show the hull capsized at the pier."

- Confirmed: CNN — "Ukraine sank a Russian warship docked in Libya… The warship — identified as the 344-foot navy corvette Merkury — was attacked with seven maritime drones… Ukraine's second target was the MV Angara cargo ship." Reuters (23 Nov) and AP report the same: seven naval drones, Merkury sunk per Zelenskyy, Angara hit, Ka-27 helicopter destroyed.
- **Problem — "capsized":** the WSJ's satellite-image description is "the warship smoldering and partially submerged in the water at the port city of Benghazi" — not capsized. No source found confirms the hull capsized. The page's own illustration shows a capsized-looking hull, but the caption text states it as a satellite-image fact.
- **Problem — attribution:** the sinking itself is Zelenskyy's claim; AP notes two Libyan officials said the corvette was "severely damaged", and Libyan officials told AP only two sea drones hit it. The page correctly attributes the sinking to Zelenskyy, so this part stands, but the satellite-image sentence should not overstate the visual evidence.

**Fix:** change "satellite images show the hull capsized at the pier" to "satellite images show the hull smoldering and partly submerged at the pier".
